CERTIFICATION UNDER 37 C.F.R. 1.29(a) -- GROSS INCOME BASIS
======================================================================

I hereby certify the following for the purpose of establishing
Micro Entity status under 37 C.F.R. 1.29(a):

(1) APPLICANT QUALIFIES AS SMALL ENTITY

    I qualify as a small entity as defined in 37 C.F.R. 1.27:
    [ ] I am an independent inventor
    [ ] I have not assigned, granted, conveyed, or licensed
        (and am not under obligation to do so) any rights in
        the invention to any person or entity that would not
        qualify as a small entity

(2) APPLICATION LIMIT

    I have not been named as an inventor on more than four (4)
    previously filed patent applications (whether provisional,
    non-provisional, international, or plant), excluding:
    - Applications filed in another country
    - Provisional applications under 35 U.S.C. 111(b)
    - Applications assigned to a previous employer

    Number of previously filed US applications as inventor: ____

(3) GROSS INCOME LIMIT

    Neither I, nor any other party with ownership interest in
    the application, had a gross income (as defined in Section
    61(a) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986) exceeding three
    times (3x) the median household income as reported by the
    Bureau of the Census for the preceding calendar year.

    US median household income (2024): approximately $80,610
    3x threshold: approximately $241,830

    [ ] I certify my gross income did not exceed $241,830 in
        the preceding calendar year
    [ ] No other party with ownership interest had gross income
        exceeding this threshold

(4) ASSIGNMENT RESTRICTION

    I have not assigned, granted, conveyed, or licensed (and am
    not under an obligation to do so) any rights in the
    application to an entity that had a gross income exceeding
    the threshold in paragraph (3).
