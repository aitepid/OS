ENTITY STATUS DETERMINATION
======================================================================

Micro Entity (37 C.F.R. 1.29):
  Qualification checklist:
  [ ] Not been named as inventor on more than 4 previously filed
      US patent applications (excluding provisionals)
  [ ] Gross income in previous year did not exceed 3x the
      US median household income
  [ ] Has not assigned/licensed/conveyed rights to an entity
      exceeding the gross income threshold

  If ALL boxes are checked, Micro Entity status applies.
  Fee reduction: 80% off large entity fees.

Small Entity (37 C.F.R. 1.27):
  If Micro Entity does not apply, check:
  [ ] Individual inventor
  [ ] Small business (fewer than 500 employees)
  [ ] Non-profit organization
  Fee reduction: 60% off large entity fees.
